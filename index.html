One call, from her phone to your team's list.

Safety and responsible use

Clever with calls. Careful with care.

The AI works inside rules your doctor sets, and some rules it can never break.

  • Your doctor signs off firstNothing goes live until the schedule and every call script are approved.
  • Recorded consent, easy to stopConsent is recorded at enrolment and mothers can stop the calls at any time.
  • Never discusses the baby's sexA fixed rule the AI cannot override, in line with the PCPNDT Act.
  • No diagnosis, no medicine adviceIt never suggests delaying a dose and only gives answers you have approved.
  • Flags go to clinical reviewConcerns are put in front of your team, never handled by the AI alone.
  • Honest about being AIMothers are told it's a computer voice, and that it isn't an emergency service.

This describes how Suraksha Sathi is designed. It is not legal advice, a certification or a regulatory approval.

Who is behind Suraksha Sathi

Suraksha Sathi is run by Poonyah India and founded by Deepti, who has spent 25 years in technology, 10 of them in health tech, across the UK, the US and India.

It's built for busy maternity and child-health practices, many with no patient software, where a phone call reaches mothers far better than an app. Its rules, from how a husband is greeted to how a concern is flagged, were shaped with the doctors at our first pilot hospital.
