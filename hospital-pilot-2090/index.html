Built for compliance

Every rule, and how it is met

We would rather show you each safeguard than ask you to trust a label. Where something must be confirmed before your first real call, we say so.

PCPNDT ActSex determination

A fixed rule the AI cannot override: it never discusses or reveals the baby's sex.

In the design

DPDP Act 2023Consent and patient rights

Consent recorded in Hindi at registration. Mothers can stop calls at any time. Data is used only for her reminders. Every staff action is logged.

In the design

Telemedicine Practice GuidelinesClinical advice

No diagnosis, no medicine advice, never suggests delaying a dose. Anything outside your approved answers goes to your team.

In the design

Clinical sign-offYour control

Nothing goes live until you sign off the schedule and every word of the call script.

In the design

Data in IndiaStorage and processing

Patient records sit in Google Cloud's Mumbai region. The voice provider's India-only processing agreement is signed before any real mother is called.

Confirmed before launch

Calling rulesTRAI and courtesy

Calls only from 10am to 7pm, from a registered number, with one retry and then WhatsApp where the mother has agreed.

The pilot

Small, simple and measured

  1. Week 0

    Your sign-off

    You approve the schedule and the call script, and share 3–6 months of past vaccine records as a baseline.

  2. Weeks 1–3

    Build and test

    You hear test calls yourself before any mother does.

  3. Launch

    First 10 mothers

    Calls begin and your daily list goes live.

  4. Then

    Grow to 50

    WhatsApp follow-up is added for mothers who agree.

  5. Months 2–3

    Results

    We review the numbers together and write the case study.

You decide at each step whether to continue. You can stop at any time, at no cost.
